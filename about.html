Why Bits of Embedded?

Embedded engineering sits at the intersection of software, hardware, electronics, mathematics and systems engineering.

Bits of Embedded is designed as a practical knowledge base where these concepts can be explored through tutorials, projects, experiments and technical notes.

Tutorials

Real engineering projects involving embedded systems, FPGA, DSP, sensor fusion and AI.

Small experiments designed to understand how hardware and software behave in real systems.
