About Bits of Embedded

Bits of Embedded is a technical learning and project platform focused on understanding, building and experimenting with embedded systems and digital engineering.

What you will find here

Step-by-step tutorials, engineering projects, experiments, implementation notes, source code and practical explanations.

Topics

Embedded Systems • FPGA • DSP • C/C++ • Sensor Fusion • AI/ML • Digital Systems

Goal

Learn the fundamentals, build practical implementations and document the complete process from concept to working system.
